Sarvam AI assistant for NVDA, version 2.1

Purpose: Sarvam AI assistant lets you use the Sarvam AI platform from NVDA, by keyboard and screen reader. It covers speech, languages, documents, AI reasoning, an agent that does work for you, and AI vision.

Where to find it: Open the NVDA menu (NVDA+N) → Tools → Sarvam AI. You can assign your own shortcuts under Preferences → Input Gestures → Sarvam AI. Settings are in that menu and under NVDA Preferences → Settings → Sarvam AI.

What is new in 2.1

  • Dubbing: dub a video or audio file into 12 Indian languages, keeping the original voices.
  • NVDA+Alt+A describes the current window with AI vision.

What is new in 2.0

  • Model switching for every feature. Sarvam 105B plus Sarvam's hosted open models: GLM 5.3, GLM 5.3 Flash, Gemma 4, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, and (with Provisioned Throughput) DeepSeek V4 Pro, Kimi K3.1 Pro and Qwen 3.8.
  • Bulbul v4 Flash persona voices, chosen by language, gender and purpose; Saaras v4 speech recognition.
  • Sarvam Agent, which does multi-step work for you.
  • AI vision for images and the screen.
  • Sarvam Document AI for reading documents and extracting data.
  • Whole-document translation that keeps the file format.
  • Upload almost any file, and download in the format you want.

Retired Sarvam models (sarvam-30b, sarvam-m, bulbul:v2, saarika and the old speech translate endpoint) were removed. Your 1.x settings are moved to the new models automatically.

Getting started

  1. Open NVDA menu → Tools → Sarvam AI → Settings…
  2. Paste your Sarvam API key (from https://dashboard.sarvam.ai), or press Generate API key to open the Sarvam developer portal.
  3. Press Validate key / Test connection, then Save.
  4. Optional: open Model switching and press Check which models my key can use.

Model switching

One list for each feature: AI Workforce and Agent, image and screen description, text to speech, speech to text, translation, and documents. Moving through a list speaks a description of the model: what it is good at, how much it can read at once, whether it sees images and its reasoning levels. Check which models my key can use marks models that your key cannot use. Press Save to keep your choices. A gesture "Switches to the next Sarvam AI chat model" changes the chat model from anywhere and announces it.

Text to Speech

Type or paste text, or press Upload file. Almost any document works, and PDFs, scans and audio are converted to text first. Then choose a voice in steps. Each step narrows the next one:

  1. Voice model: Bulbul v4 Flash (222 persona voices) or Bulbul v3 (37 voices that speak every language).
  2. Language: Auto detect, or a language. Bulbul v4 Flash personas are recorded per language. Malayalam and Odia use Bulbul v3, and the add-on switches to it automatically and says so.
  3. Gender: Female or Male.
  4. Purpose (nature of work): Conversational; Narration, stories and audiobooks; News and documentary; Customer support, banking and business; Education and children; Ads, sales and social media; Devotional; Drama and characters; Companion and assistant; Sports; Numbers and announcements; Formal; Expressive. Each entry says how many voices it has. Choosing a purpose also sets suitable speed and expression.
  5. Voice: read as, for example, "Ritu - Hindi - Banking (female)".

Adjust Speech speed and Expression if you like, then press Convert. Then use Listen, or choose a Download format (MP3, WAV, FLAC or AAC) and press Download audio.

Speech to Text

Press Choose audio file (WAV, MP3, M4A, MP4, AAC, AIFF, FLAC, OGG, Opus, WebM, AMR, WMA) or Start recording. Choose the audio language and the Output: automatic, transcribe in the spoken language, translate to English, verbatim, romanised, or code-mixed. Optionally:

  • Key terms: names or jargon to spell correctly, separated by commas (Saaras v4).
  • Separate speakers: produces "Speaker 1: …" lines.
  • Include timings: lets you download SRT subtitles.

Press Transcribe. Long audio, up to two hours, is processed as a batch job automatically.

Mixed-language speech: leave the language on Auto and the output on Automatic. The Kannada stays in Kannada script and the English words stay in English, for example "ನಾನು ಢಂದು office ಗೆ ಹೋಗಬೆಕು because I have a meeting."

Translate, Transliterate, Detect language

Enter text, or press Upload file to read the text of any supported file. Choose the Operation, the Translation model and the languages, and press Go. Sarvam-Translate offers all 22 scheduled Indian languages. Mayura offers formal, colloquial and code-mixed styles.

Translate a document file and keep its format sends a PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, HTML, Markdown or text file to Sarvam. The translated copy comes back in the same format, with its layout kept, and is saved in the folder you choose. Set the source language first; Auto detect is not accepted here.

Documents and OCR

Press Choose PDF, image or ZIP, then choose the Task:

  • Read the document: Sarvam Document AI recognises the text in 23 languages and keeps headings, tables, columns and reading order.
  • Extract data into a spreadsheet: describe what you need in plain words, for example "invoice number, date, seller, and each line item with quantity and price". The chat model turns this into an extraction plan, Document AI fills it in, and Download the spreadsheet saves an Excel, CSV or JSON file.

From the result you can go on to Translate, Read aloud, or the AI Workforce.

AI Workforce and the Sarvam Agent

The cursor starts in Ask a question. Shift+Tab reaches Attach files, where you can attach several files of any type, including images. Ctrl+Enter runs the task. Choose:

  • Task mode: AI Assistant, Agent, Describe images and screenshots, AI Summary, Document Analysis, Content Generation, Question Answering, General AI Workforce.
  • Model and Reasoning: the reasoning list shows only the levels the chosen model accepts.

Follow-up questions keep the conversation. New conversation starts over.

Agent mode lets the model act for you. It can read your attached files, translate, transliterate, detect languages, create speech files with the right voice for a gender and purpose, and save documents in any format. For example: "Translate the attached letter into Kannada, save it as a Word file, and make an MP3 of it read by a female narration voice." Each step is announced and listed under Agent steps. The agent can read only the files you attached and can only create new files in your output folder. It never overwrites or deletes files, and it cannot run programs.

Dubbing

Open Tools → Sarvam AI → Dubbing and press Choose video or audio file. Video can be MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI, FLV or WMV, and audio can be WAV, MP3 or M4A. Then set:

  • Spoken language in the file: Auto detect, or one of the Indian or international languages.
  • Dub into: a list of 12 languages (English, Hindi, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Assamese). Press Space to check each language you want.
  • Files to get back: dubbed video (MP4), dubbed audio (WAV) and/or subtitles (SRT).
  • Clone the original speakers' voices (on by default) and the number of speakers, with 0 to detect them. With cloning off, type a preset voice name, for example ishita, and choose a pace.
  • Language style, and whether to remove the video watermark.

Press Dub. The add-on states the cost: about 40 rupees of Sarvam credit per minute of media for each language. It asks you to confirm, then asks where to save. Dubbing can take several minutes. When it finishes, one file per language and format is saved, named like talk_kn-IN.mp4.

AI vision: images and the screen

Attach a photo or screenshot in AI Workforce and choose Describe images and screenshots. If the chosen model cannot see images, the vision model from Model switching (Gemma 4 by default) is used for that request.

Press NVDA+Alt+A anywhere to describe the current window, including all visible text. The menu item does the same, and an assignable gesture "Describes the whole screen" describes the entire screen. The description opens in AI Workforce, so you can ask follow-up questions about what is on screen.

Downloading results

When a task finishes, a Download format list and a Download button appear. The formats are Word (.docx), Markdown, web page (.html), rich text (.rtf), Excel (.xlsx), CSV, JSON, plain text, and SRT subtitles for timed transcripts. Tables become real tables in Word, HTML and Excel. The add-on remembers the format you used last.

Keyboard shortcuts

NVDA+Alt+A describes the current window with AI vision. It is the only shortcut assigned by default, and NVDA itself does not use it. Change it, or assign your own keys, in NVDA → Preferences → Input Gestures → Sarvam AI, for example for the Sarvam AI menu, Dubbing, Text to Speech, Speech to Text, Translate, OCR, AI Workforce, the Agent, Model switching, describing the window or the whole screen, or switching to the next chat model. Inside any Sarvam AI window, Escape closes it, even from inside a text box.

Troubleshooting

SymptomCause / fix
"No Sarvam API key set"Enter your key in Settings.
"Authentication failed"The key is wrong; re-check it.
"No credits available"Your account needs credits. Add them in the Sarvam dashboard.
"The model … is not available to your Sarvam key"Choose another model in Model switching. Models marked Provisioned need Provisioned Throughput on your Sarvam account.
"The model ran out of output space"Lower the reasoning level, or raise the maximum response length in Advanced settings.
Document reading failsIf Document AI is not enabled for your key, the add-on falls back to the older OCR API automatically. You can also choose the legacy engine in Model switching.
Nothing happens on the networkCheck your connection, proxy and network timeout in Settings. Open Logs for details.

Privacy

Your API key is stored obfuscated in NVDA's configuration. The text, audio, images and documents you process are sent to Sarvam for processing. The screen is captured only when you ask for a description. Open models are hosted by Sarvam, so those requests also go only to Sarvam.
